# Voice questionnaire

Questionnaire version: **1**

This is a reusable question bank, not a form to fill in within this repository.
Store answers and examples only in the selected local profile. The default is
learning through usage; a user can choose a deeper interview or pause it.

## Conducting the interview

For a deeper interview, start with Q01–Q03, then choose small batches relevant
to the user's writing. For usage-based learning, ask one relevant question when
it would resolve an actual drafting choice. Let the user skip any question.
Use contrasts rather than adjectives alone: two sentences with identical facts
and different tone are easier to judge than a request for “brand attributes.”

Distinguish an answer about current habits from an instruction about future
writing. Confirm your synthesis before promoting inferred preferences to rules.
Explicit preferences can be recorded immediately. Mark a question answered only
when the user supplied an answer; an inference is not an answer.

## Speaker and intent

- **Q01 — Ownership:** Is this your personal voice or a project/organization's
  voice? Who is speaking when the profile applies?
- **Q02 — Desired impression:** What should readers think or feel about the
  speaker? Which impressions should the writing avoid? How does that differ
  from how your writing comes across today?
- **Q03 — Contexts:** Who do you write to, through which media, and for what
  purposes? Which combinations should sound different?

## Natural voice and deliberate choices

- **Q04 — Samples:** Can you provide a short piece you wrote that sounds like
  you, and one that does not? What distinguishes them? May a sanitized excerpt
  be retained locally, or should we retain only the resulting rules?
- **Q05 — Rhythm:** Do you favor short sentences, longer flowing sentences, or
  a mix? How much explanation should precede an answer or request?
- **Q06 — Relationship:** How do warmth, formality, and directness change with
  a friend, colleague, unfamiliar reader, or customer? Show a preferred request.
- **Q07 — Vocabulary:** Which expressions feel natural or artificial? What
  should happen with jargon, acronyms, abbreviations, and contractions?
- **Q08 — Mechanics:** What are your preferences for punctuation, capitalization,
  spelling locale, emoji, and emphasis? Are any medium-specific?
- **Q09 — Difficult messages:** How should the voice decline, disagree,
  apologize, express uncertainty, or deliver bad news? What must it avoid?
- **Q10 — Personality:** When are humor, enthusiasm, storytelling, or personal
  details welcome? Where would they feel inappropriate?

## Medium and audience preferences

- **Q11 — Email:** How should greetings, sign-offs, paragraph length, and lists
  differ between informal, professional, and formal email? For Gmail or Outlook,
  does the actual composing tool insert a signature, or must one be supplied?
- **Q12 — Team chat:** How should Teams and Slack messages differ from email?
  When do you prefer one paragraph, a list, a longer explanation, or a thread?
- **Q13 — Text messages:** How brief should texts be for different audiences?
  Are greetings, emoji, and multiple messages welcome? One message is the
  delivery default unless the user requests otherwise.
- **Q14 — Documents:** What voice should introductions, explanations, headings,
  and recommendations use? Which document conventions are already established?
- **Q15 — Public posts:** Where might you post, who is the audience, and what
  impression should the voice give? How do you feel about calls to action,
  hashtags, personal stories, and promotional language? Keep platform formatting
  undecided until the platform is known.

## Calibration and learning

- **Q16 — Compare:** Given two drafts with the same facts, which sounds closer
  to your intent, and what would you change? Treat the answer as evidence about
  the named context, not a universal rule without confirmation.
- **Q17 — Exceptions:** Which preferences apply everywhere, which belong only
  to a medium or audience, and which apply only to this draft?
- **Q18 — Retention:** Are any details or examples off limits for the saved
  profile? Would you like to review or remove anything already captured?

## Maintaining this questionnaire

Keep question IDs stable. For a substantive change to what a question learns,
add the next versioned questionnaire file, document changes there, and update
the skill's pointer. Retain earlier versions for interpreting saved question
IDs. Editorial clarifications can remain in the same version. Never add a real
user's answers to a questionnaire, fixture, or example.
