# Public posts

Formatting reference version: **1**

Public is an audience, not a single transport. Apply confirmed public-post voice
preferences for tone, disclosure, promotion, and calls to action. Do not infer
hashtags, emoji, sales language, or engagement tactics from the word “public.”

Until a platform is named, provide platform-neutral plain text with paragraph
breaks and identify it as a draft. Once named, inspect its editor or tool for
supported formatting, length limits, link handling, and edit support. Do not
assume Markdown, HTML, or a platform-specific character limit. Avoid decorative
Unicode characters used to simulate bold text.

If the post exceeds a platform limit, propose a shorter version or ask about a
thread. Shortening changes wording and splitting changes delivery; neither is
a formatting-only operation. Confirm the intended account and audience before
an authorized publication. No automatic posting or replacement post on failure.
